# SurgicGuide Mobile — Privacy Notice

**Effective date: 26 October 2026**

SurgicGuide Mobile is an academic and illustrative application for a hypothetical company and hypothetical AI-integrated FLIM System.

## Data collection

Version 1.0 does not require an account and does not intentionally collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal data, health data, patient data, photographs, precise location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or analytics data.

The simulated FLIM viewer uses fixed illustrative values generated within the app. It does not accept or analyse patient images or medical records.

## External website

The app can open the SurgicGuide website in the device's browser. Once the user leaves the app, the destination website and browser environment may operate under their own privacy and technical policies.

The Team image is bundled with the application and does not require a network request.

## Medical status

The app is not a medical device and is not intended for diagnosis, treatment, surgical guidance, clinical decision-making, or emergency use.

## Changes

If future versions add accounts, analytics, uploads, cloud processing, real image analysis, or other networked features, this notice and the relevant app-store privacy disclosures must be updated before release.
