A Day at SurgicGuide: Steve’s Perspective

SurgicGuide / 26 Sept 2026

Hi everyone! I’m Steve (Lam, Siu-yu), VP of Product Development at SurgicGuide.

Ever wondered what a typical day looks like at SurgicGuide? Honestly, there isn’t really a “typical” day!

My work usually involves moving between product development, system testing, data analysis, and plenty of discussions with the team. One moment I might be looking at FLIM imaging data, and the next I’m discussing how we can make our AI-integrated FLIM System more practical and reliable for real-world surgical applications.

A big part of my job is connecting the technical side with the bigger picture. We constantly ask ourselves: Does this work? Can we make it better? And, most importantly, can we turn it into something that can genuinely support surgeons and patients?

Of course, not every day is about breakthroughs. There are plenty of debugging sessions, unexpected results, repeated experiments, and meetings that run longer than expected! But that’s also what makes product development exciting.

At the end of the day, seeing an idea gradually become a working technology is incredibly rewarding. We’re still building, testing, and learning—but that’s exactly what makes the journey exciting.

— Steve

Lam, Siu-yu
