# SurgicGuide design guide

## Visual language

A restrained navy-and-teal palette, white backgrounds and a consistent spacing scale keep the focus on the scientific content. Poppins headings are paired with a readable body typeface. Navigation, page introductions and the footer share a common alignment and width.

## Scientific content

Long-form pages use a reading column with an adjacent contents list on desktop. On smaller screens, the contents list sits above the article. Paragraphs are left aligned and retain semantic emphasis, citations and chemical notation.

Each scientific figure has a descriptive alternative text, a purpose-specific size and an enlarged-image view. Figures preserve their natural proportions. Related microscopy images are paired, while metabolic diagrams use a dedicated arrangement. Captions are distinct from the surrounding scientific discussion.

## Images and cards

Team portraits use consistent frames and crops. Both partner sheets are visible. Journal cards use a consistent cover ratio and flexible text areas so titles remain readable in full.

## Accessibility

The site includes keyboard-accessible menus, visible focus indicators, a skip link, labelled forms, reduced-motion support and focus return after closing the figure viewer. Every page displays an academic-project notice with the complete disclaimer available through a disclosure.

## Verification

All 20 public pages are checked at 320, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 pixels. Automated checks cover local assets, horizontal overflow, heading structure, figure proportions, menus, image viewing, journal search, reference numbering and form validation.
