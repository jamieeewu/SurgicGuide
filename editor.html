SurgicGuide journal

Your writing space.

Write and edit articles using Pages CMS. Sign in with your GitHub account and choose jamieeewu / SurgicGuide, then Journal posts.

Open the editor ↗

Publish an article

  1. Create a post and add its title, date, cover photo, summary and article text.
  2. Use a filename with lowercase words separated by hyphens. This becomes the article’s address; keep existing filenames unchanged to preserve links.
  3. Turn on Publish on website and save. The website rebuilds automatically; allow a few minutes.

Saving with the switch off keeps the post out of the website, but all saved text and uploaded images are public on GitHub. Keep confidential drafts in a private writing app.

Your GitHub account controls editing access. Visitors cannot publish articles.

On iPhone, bookmark the editor or use Safari’s Share menu to add it to your Home Screen.
