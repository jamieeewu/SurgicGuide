From first word to published post
  1. Add your title, date, cover image and article.
  2. Insert photos using the article’s image picker and wait for uploads to finish.
  3. Turn on Publish on website and save. Your story goes live after the site rebuilds.

Keep existing filenames unchanged when editing a published article to preserve its address.

A note about unpublished writing

The publishing switch controls whether your post appears on the website. Saved text and images remain public on GitHub, even with the switch off. Keep confidential drafts in a private writing app.
